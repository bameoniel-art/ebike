The foundation

Four rules. Every time.

Treat every firearm with care, whether it is familiar or not. These habits are a starting point, not a substitute for hands-on instruction.

01 ↗

Keep it pointed
in a safe direction.

Be aware of where it is pointed at all times. Choose a direction where an unintended discharge would not injure anyone.

02 ⌁

Keep your finger
off the trigger.

Keep your finger outside the trigger guard until you are ready to shoot in a controlled, lawful setting.

03 ◎

Know your target
and what is beyond.

Never fire at a sound or movement. Be certain of your target and the area beyond it before taking any shot.

04 ▣

Store it locked
and inaccessible.

When not in use, secure firearms so children, visitors, and anyone unauthorized cannot access them.

! If you are unsure how to handle a firearm safely, do not handle it. Ask a qualified instructor for guidance.

SECUREEVERY TIME

Safety is a daily practice, not a one-time decision.

At home

Make secure
storage the default.

A firearm should be inaccessible to anyone who is not authorized to use it. A locked container and a consistent routine add meaningful layers of protection.

  • 01

    Use a locked safe or secure lockbox designed for firearm storage.

  • 02

    Keep keys and combinations inaccessible to children and unauthorized people.

  • 03

    Store ammunition separately, in a locked location.

  • 04

    Review access whenever your household, visitors, or circumstances change.
